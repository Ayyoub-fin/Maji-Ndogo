Key Highlights
Real-Time Water Access Overview
Identify towns relying on wells, home taps, shared taps, or rivers. This section displays the specific number of people served by each water source.

Population Breakdown
Perform rapid comparisons between rural and urban populations to help customize water-supply strategies.

Crime Insights
Investigate who is impacted by different offenses, determine when incidents peak, and locate where they occur.

Improvement Projects Tracker
Track ongoing work dedicated to the expansion or maintenance of water systems.

📊 What You’ll Find
Town-Level Water Profiles
Stacked bar charts illustrate the water supply mix for every community. This data is used to identify neighborhoods requiring new wells or tap repairs.

Population Distribution
A simple pie chart highlights the difference between rural and urban residency. Use this to direct resources to areas with the highest impact.

Water Source Coverage
A treemap indicates the number of residents dependent on each water source type, categorized by province.

Source Counts
Bar charts provide a census of wells, working taps, broken taps, shared taps, and rivers to show which infrastructure is most common.

Crime Patterns
A gender-segmented pie chart displays total incidents by category, such as theft, vandalism, or assault. Clustered bars provide more detail on gender trends for each crime per province.

Timing of Incidents
Line charts show offenses by hour and day of the week, including overlays for male and female victims. This allows you to see high-risk periods immediately.
